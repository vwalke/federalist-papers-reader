The Weekly Course

One letter a week, on the morning of your choosing—the eighty-five papers and the eight essays that answered them, in the order the debate reached readers, for ninety-three weeks. Your first, Brutus No. I, arrives the morning after you confirm, so everyone begins in the same place, whenever they join.

As It Happened

Each paper and essay arrives on the anniversary of its original 1787–88 publication—in fits and starts, just as New Yorkers first met it. The season opens October 18 with Brutus No. I and closes April 26, with the eight papers of the McLean’s Edition finale delivered every three days through April.
